About

I am a Ph.D. student at Mila and the Université de Montréal, where I am co-advised by Professors Glen Berseth and Marc G. Bellemare. Previously, I received my M.S. in Computer Science at Case Western Reserve University, where I was advised by Professor Soumya Ray.
